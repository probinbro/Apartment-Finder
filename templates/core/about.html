About {{ SITE_NAME }}

We make finding a rental home simple, transparent and stress-free.

Our mission

All apartment information, in one place

In Bangladesh, finding a flat usually means walking the streets looking for To-Let signs, calling numbers from Facebook groups and visiting places whose rent, advance or service charge turn out to be different from what you were told.

{{ SITE_NAME }} brings every listing into one organized marketplace. Each apartment shows its rent, size, rooms, amenities, photos and a direct contact, so you can compare options quickly and reach out with confidence.

How it works

Browse & search. Anyone can explore listings and filter by location, budget, bedrooms and property type.

Create a free account. Registered users get a personal dashboard with saved apartments and their bookings.

Book online. Send a booking request with your move-in date. The flat is held for you, and the landlord's number is shared as soon as they accept.

List your flat. Landlords list for free, get booking requests by email and manage everything from their dashboard.
